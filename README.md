#### Navlonix — freight marketplace

<img src="assets/shots/navlonix.jpg" alt="Navlonix landing page" width="100%">

Businesses that need to move cargo post a load; verified drivers send offers; the job is tracked until delivery is confirmed. It replaces the phone calls, haggling and cash risk that most domestic freight still runs on. Web, mobile and an admin panel, in pilot stage.

[navlonix.com](https://navlonix.com)

<br>

#### Nilüfer İlaçlama — field-service operations

<img src="assets/shots/nilufer-ilaclama.jpg" alt="Nilüfer İlaçlama website" width="100%">

A pest-control company in Bursa runs its customers, field crews, contracts and payments from one system instead of spreadsheets and phone calls. Public website, office panel and a mobile app for staff in the field.
